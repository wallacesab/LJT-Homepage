I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, and I am interested in understanding and improving large language models.

A full list of my publications is available on the [Publications]({{ site.baseurl }}/publications/) page, and a summary of my education, research experience, and publications is on my [CV]({{ site.baseurl }}/cv/).

Research interests
======
* Large language model reasoning and reinforcement learning
* Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
* LLM truthfulness and interpretability
